Smart Locker Pickup

Contactless pickup. Order ahead, collect anytime.

What is Smart Locker Pickup?

Order through our app. We’ll prepare your drinks and store them in a secure, temperature-controlled locker. You’ll receive a QR code or PIN to unlock when you’re ready to collect — even outside regular hours.

Never used Locker Pickup before?

Everything you need to know in under 60 seconds.

1

Order Online

Browse our menu and place your order. Select Locker Pickup at checkout and choose a location.

2

We Prepare It

Our baristas craft your order fresh, then place it in a temperature-controlled locker (hot stays hot, cold stays cold).

3

Scan & Collect

You’ll get a QR code & backup PIN. Scan or enter the PIN at the touchscreen, collect, and go.

How to collect your order

  1. Go to the Smart Locker at your selected location.
  2. Scan the QR code or enter the PIN on the touchscreen.
  3. Your locker door will unlock automatically — collect your order and close the door.
