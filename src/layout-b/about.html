About Us

Crafted with Care, Served with Heart

We started as a small pop-up with one espresso machine and a big dream: share beautiful coffee and fresh bakes with our community. Today, Steam & Sugar is a neighbourhood favorite,still independent, still obsessed with the details.

Our Roastery Partners

Ethically Sourced. Roasted to Shine.

We partner with small farms and trusted roasters who pay fair prices and prioritise sustainability. Every lot is profiled to highlight its unique character, from chocolatey Brazilian naturals to bright, floral Ethiopians.

Our Bakery

Made Fresh Every Morning

Croissants laminated in-house, brownies with real cocoa, and seasonal tarts, everything baked in small batches so it tastes like it just left the oven.
