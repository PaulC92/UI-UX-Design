What is the Smart Cup System?

Our RFID-enabled smart cups let you bring your own reusable mug and earn rewards every time. Each cup has a unique chip that tracks your usage, calculates your environmental impact, and automatically applies discounts.

New to Smart Reusable Cups?

Here’s how it works in 3 simple steps

1. Get Your Cup

Purchase a Steam & Sugar smart cup for £15 (one-time) or register your own reusable mug with an RFID tag.

2. Tap & Order

Bring your cup to the counter or scan in the app. Our system recognizes it instantly and applies your discount.

3. Earn & Track

Get £0.50 off every drink and watch your impact grow. Track CO₂ saved and waste reduced.
