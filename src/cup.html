What is the Smart Cup system?

Our RFID-enabled smart cups let you bring your own reusable mug and earn rewards every time. Each cup has a unique chip that tracks your usage, calculates environmental impact, and automatically applies discounts at checkout.

How it works

1. Get Your Cup

Buy a Steam & Sugar smart cup (£15 one-time) or register your own mug with an RFID tag.

2. Tap & Order

Tap at the counter or in-app; the system recognizes your mug and applies the discount.

3. Earn & Track

Save £0.50 on every drink and track CO₂ saved & waste reduced in your activity.
